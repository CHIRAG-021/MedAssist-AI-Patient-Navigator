📌 Adherence Tips
⏰
Set daily alarms on your phone as medication reminders — consistency is key.
🗓️
Use a weekly pill organizer (pill box) to pre-fill your weekly medications every Sunday.

👨‍👩‍👧

📋 Age-based Health Screening Guide
👶
Children (0–12 years): Follow National Immunization Schedule (BCG, Polio, DPT, MMR). Annual growth monitoring and vision check.
🧑
Teens (13–19 years): Anaemia screening for girls. HPV vaccine. Dental checkup. Mental health awareness.
👨
Adults (20–40 years): Annual BP & blood sugar check. Lipid profile every 3 years. Eye exam every 2 years.
🧔
Middle-aged (40–60 years): HbA1c, ECG, full thyroid panel, Vitamin D & B12 annually. Colonoscopy at 50.
👴
Seniors (60+ years): Bone density scan (DEXA). Cardiac screening. Memory assessment. Flu & pneumonia vaccines. Fall prevention.
